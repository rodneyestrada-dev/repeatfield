02 / Workflows

Three ways
to make.

Each workflow receives the same visual family but keeps its own mental model. The cards are generous, quickly scannable, and differentiated through diagrams instead of decorative icons.

03 / Components

Friendly tools.
Honest states.

Roundness is structural, not ornamental: 14px controls, 20px utility cards, 30px product cards, and 42px large sections. Motion is short and tactile; diagnostics remain literal.
