04 / FAQ

Good
to know.

Repeatfield is scoped honestly on purpose. Here is what it is, what it does with your images, and where its current limits sit.

Do my images leave my browser?

No. Repeatfield has no backend, no account, and no upload. Cropping, tiling, and exports all happen locally, and projects persist in your browser's own storage.

What can I export?

Framed PNG in square, portrait, or landscape, or custom dimensions — rendered by the same engine you preview with, so what you see is what ships. Tessellate exports transparent PNG.

What are the current limits?

Selection is quadrilateral (a four-point lasso), background removal is color-distance based, and tessellation is assisted manual placement — honest tools, honestly scoped.

What does it cost?

Nothing. Repeatfield is free and browser-local — open the studio and start making.
